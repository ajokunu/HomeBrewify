# Chapter 1: Nested Content

> The chamber opens before you, vast and echoing. Torchlight flickers against walls covered in ancient murals depicting a war between gods.

---

**DM Note:** This room contains a complex puzzle. The players must arrange the murals in the correct order (see table below) to open the sealed door. A DC 18 Investigation check reveals that the murals can be rotated.

---

## Location: The Hall of Murals

An immense underground gallery with twelve rotating mural panels set into the walls.

### NPC: The Curator

*Human ghost, neutral*

**Personality.** The Curator speaks in a monotone voice and obsessively corrects anyone who misidentifies the murals.
**Ideals.** Preservation. Art must be maintained for eternity.
**Bonds.** Bound to the gallery until someone correctly restores all twelve murals.
**Flaws.** Cannot remember the correct arrangement of the murals, despite claiming otherwise.

> "Do not touch the murals with your bare hands. The oils from your skin will... well, I suppose it hardly matters now."
> — The Curator, *Ghost of the Gallery*

# Chapter 2: Complex Monster

### Monster: The Curator (Combat Form)

*Medium undead, neutral*

AC: 13
HP: 45 (10d8)
Speed: 0 ft., fly 40 ft. (hover)

STR 1 (-5) | DEX 16 (+3) | CON 10 (+0) | INT 18 (+4) | WIS 14 (+2) | CHA 16 (+3)

**Saving Throws:** Int +7, Wis +5
**Skills:** Arcana +7, History +10, Perception +5
**Damage Resistances:** acid, fire, lightning, thunder; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks
**Damage Immunities:** cold, necrotic, poison
**Condition Immunities:** charmed, exhaustion, frightened, grappled, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, prone, restrained
**Senses:** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 15
**Languages:** Common, Elvish, Dwarvish, Celestial
**Challenge:** 5 (1,800 XP)

**Incorporeal Movement.** The Curator can move through other creatures and objects as if they were difficult terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.

**Ethereal Sight.** The Curator can see 60 feet into the Ethereal Plane when it is on the Material Plane, and vice versa.

**Gallery Bond.** The Curator cannot willingly move more than 100 feet from the Hall of Murals. If forced beyond this range, it teleports back to the center of the gallery.

***Spellcasting.*** The Curator is a 10th-level spellcaster. Its spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save DC 15, +7 to hit with spell attacks). The Curator has the following spells prepared:

Cantrips (at will): *mage hand, minor illusion, prestidigitation*
1st level (4 slots): *color spray, silent image, shield*
2nd level (3 slots): *blur, mirror image, phantasmal force*
3rd level (3 slots): *hypnotic pattern, major image*
4th level (3 slots): *greater invisibility, phantasmal killer*
5th level (2 slots): *mislead, wall of force*

### Actions

**Withering Touch.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 17 (4d6 + 3) necrotic damage.

**Horrifying Visage.** Each non-undead creature within 60 feet of the Curator that can see it must succeed on a DC 14 Wisdom saving throw or be frightened for 1 minute. If the save fails by 5 or more, the target also ages 1d4 x 10 years. A frightened creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, with disadvantage if the Curator is within line of sight, ending the frightened condition on a success.

### Reactions

**Mural Defense.** When the Curator takes damage while within the Hall of Murals, it can use its reaction to animate one of the mural panels. The mural intercepts the attack, reducing the damage by 10 (3d6). The mural panel is then destroyed.

# Chapter 3: Items with Special Properties

## Magic Item: Palette of Illusions

*Wondrous item, very rare (requires attunement by a spellcaster)*

This painter's palette is carved from petrified wood and set with seven gemstone paints of different colors. While attuned, you gain the following benefits:

- You gain proficiency with painter's supplies if you don't already have it.
- You can cast *minor illusion* at will, and the illusion appears as a painted image floating in the air.
- The palette has 7 charges. You can use an action to expend charges to cast one of the following spells from it, using your spell save DC:
  - *silent image* (1 charge)
  - *phantasmal force* (2 charges)
  - *major image* (3 charges)
  - *hallucinatory terrain* (4 charges)
  - *programmed illusion* (5 charges)

The palette regains 1d4 + 3 expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the palette's colors fade and it becomes a nonmagical painter's palette.

**Curse.** After creating an illusion with the palette, you must succeed on a DC 12 Wisdom saving throw or become convinced the illusion is real for 1 minute.

## Magic Item: Mural Fragment

*Wondrous item, rare*

This fragment of an ancient mural radiates faint magical energy. You can use an action to press the fragment against a flat surface at least 5 feet wide and 5 feet tall. The fragment creates a lifelike mural on the surface depicting a scene of your choice. The mural is permanent but nonmagical.

If the fragment is pressed against a wall while you speak a command word, the mural becomes a portal to a 10-foot cube extradimensional space for 1 hour. The space is furnished with comfortable chairs and a small table. When the duration ends, any creatures inside the space are expelled to the nearest unoccupied space. This property can be used once per day.
