# The Lost Temple

## A Level 3-5 Adventure

*A classic dungeon crawl for 4-5 players*

---

## Adventure Background

Deep in the forest lies an ancient temple, long forgotten by civilization. Recent earthquakes have revealed its entrance, and rumors of treasure have drawn adventurers to the region.

## Chapter 1: The Village

> The small village of Millbrook appears peaceful at first glance. Smoke rises from chimneys, and the smell of fresh bread wafts from the bakery. But the villagers eye you with suspicion—strangers are rare here.

### Key NPCs

**Elder Mira** - Village leader, wise but cautious
- Knows the temple's history
- Will hire the party to investigate

**Blacksmith Torvald** - Gruff but helpful
- Can provide equipment
- Lost his brother to the temple years ago

---

## Chapter 2: The Temple

### General Features

**Lighting:** Dim light from glowing fungi
**Ceilings:** 15 feet high
**Walls:** Ancient stone covered in moss

### Room 1: Entry Hall

> Crumbling pillars line this long hallway. The air is thick with dust and the smell of decay. At the far end, a statue of a forgotten god watches with empty eyes.

**Trap:** Pressure plate triggers poison darts (DC 13 Perception to notice, DC 12 Dexterity save or 2d6 poison damage)

### Room 2: Guardian Chamber

A stone guardian protects this room.

### Stone Guardian

*Medium construct, unaligned*

AC: 15 (natural armor)
HP: 45 (6d8 + 18)
Speed: 25 ft.

STR 16 (+3) | DEX 8 (-1) | CON 16 (+3) | INT 3 (-4) | WIS 10 (+0) | CHA 1 (-5)

**Damage Immunities:** poison, psychic
**Condition Immunities:** charmed, frightened, paralyzed, poisoned

**False Appearance.** While motionless, the guardian is indistinguishable from a normal statue.

### Actions

**Slam.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) bludgeoning damage.

**Rock Throw.** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, range 30/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d10 + 3) bludgeoning damage.

---

## Appendix: Magic Items

### Amulet of the Temple

*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)*

This ancient amulet bears the symbol of the temple's forgotten deity. While wearing it, you have advantage on saving throws against being frightened.

**Guiding Light.** As a bonus action, you can cause the amulet to shed bright light in a 20-foot radius and dim light for an additional 20 feet. The light lasts for 1 hour or until you dismiss it.

### Guardian's Blade

*Weapon (longsword), rare*

This stone blade was carried by the temple's ancient protectors. You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this weapon.

**Sentinel Strike.** When you hit a creature with this weapon, you can force it to make a DC 14 Strength saving throw. On a failure, its speed is reduced to 0 until the start of your next turn. You can use this property three times, regaining all uses at dawn.

---

## Conclusion

With the temple cleared, the village of Millbrook can rest easy. Elder Mira rewards the party with 500 gold pieces and the gratitude of the village.

*Adventure designed for 4-5 players*
*Estimated play time: 4-6 hours*
