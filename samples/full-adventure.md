# The Shattered Sanctum
## A Level 3-5 Adventure

*Written by the DM*

# Chapter 1: The Village of Thornwall

> The cobblestone road opens into a quaint village square. Smoke rises lazily from chimneys, and the scent of fresh bread mingles with the earthy aroma of autumn leaves. Villagers eye you with a mixture of hope and suspicion.

## Location: Thornwall Village

A modest farming village perched on the edge of the Greymist Forest. Thornwall's economy relies on timber and a small silver mine in the nearby hills.

### The Sleeping Giant Inn

> Warm light spills from the tavern windows. Inside, a crackling fire casts dancing shadows across rough-hewn beams. The innkeeper, a stout woman with laugh lines around her eyes, waves you toward an empty table.

### NPC: Marta Blackthorn

*Human innkeeper, neutral good*

**Personality.** Marta is warm and welcoming, but fiercely protective of her village.
**Ideals.** Community. We must look after our own.
**Bonds.** Her husband disappeared in the Greymist Forest two weeks ago.
**Flaws.** Refuses to believe anything supernatural could be responsible.

> "Sit, sit! You look like you've traveled far. Let me get you something warm."
> — Marta Blackthorn, *Innkeeper of the Sleeping Giant*

### NPC: Elder Tomas Greythorn

*Half-elf sage, lawful neutral*

**Personality.** Speaks in riddles and metaphors. Has forgotten more lore than most scholars will ever learn.
**Ideals.** Knowledge. The truth must be preserved, no matter how painful.
**Bonds.** Knows the secret history of the Shattered Sanctum.
**Flaws.** Paralyzed by indecision when lives are at stake.

---

**DM Note:** Elder Tomas will only reveal the location of the Sanctum if the party earns his trust through a DC 15 Persuasion check or by recovering his stolen grimoire from the bandits in Area 3.

> The forest closes in around you. Ancient trees twist overhead, their branches interlocking like skeletal fingers. A thick mist clings to the ground, muffling your footsteps and turning the world grey and formless.

### The Ruined Bridge

> The stone bridge ahead has partially collapsed. Below, dark water churns over jagged rocks. The far bank is barely visible through the mist, but you can make out a faint, flickering light.

**DM Note:** The bridge requires a DC 12 Athletics check to cross safely. On a failure, the character falls 20 feet into the river and takes 2d6 bludgeoning damage.

# Chapter 3: The Shattered Sanctum

> Before you stands a temple of black stone, half-consumed by the earth itself. Cracks run through the walls like veins, and from within, a faint, pulsing light emanates — violet and wrong, like a bruise on reality.

## Location: The Shattered Sanctum

An ancient temple dedicated to a forgotten god of twilight, now corrupted by a rift to the Shadowfell. The sanctum is partially collapsed and overgrown, but its magical wards still pulse with residual power.

### Area 1: The Entry Hall

> Shattered pews line the aisle of this once-magnificent hall. Stained glass windows, now mostly broken, still cast feeble shards of colored light across the debris-strewn floor.

**Trap.** A pressure plate (DC 14 Perception to detect) triggers a volley of poisoned darts. +6 to hit, 1d4+2 piercing damage plus 2d6 poison damage on a failed DC 13 Constitution save.

### Area 2: The Shadow Pool

> In the center of this circular chamber, a pool of liquid shadow ripples without wind. Tendrils of darkness reach upward from its surface, grasping at the air like drowning hands.

**Secret:** The shadow pool is the source of the shadow duplicates plaguing the region. It can be sealed by placing the *Amulet of Twilight* in the basin at its center.

### Monster: Shadow Sentinel

*Large undead, neutral evil*

AC: 16 (natural armor)
HP: 75 (10d10 + 20)
Speed: 30 ft., fly 40 ft. (hover)

STR 16 (+3) | DEX 14 (+2) | CON 14 (+2) | INT 10 (+0) | WIS 12 (+1) | CHA 8 (-1)

**Saving Throws:** Str +5, Con +4
**Damage Resistances:** necrotic; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks
**Damage Immunities:** poison
**Condition Immunities:** exhaustion, frightened, poisoned
**Senses:** darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 11
**Languages:** understands Common but can't speak
**Challenge:** 4 (1,100 XP)

**Shadow Stealth.** While in dim light or darkness, the sentinel can take the Hide action as a bonus action.

**Sunlight Sensitivity.** While in sunlight, the sentinel has disadvantage on attack rolls and Wisdom (Perception) checks.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The sentinel makes two shadow blade attacks.

**Shadow Blade.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 12 (2d8 + 3) necrotic damage. The target's hit point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to the necrotic damage taken. This reduction lasts until the target finishes a long rest.

**Shadow Burst (Recharge 5-6).** The sentinel releases a burst of shadow energy in a 20-foot radius. Each creature in the area must make a DC 13 Constitution saving throw, taking 18 (4d8) necrotic damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.

*Small undead, chaotic evil*

AC: 13
HP: 22 (5d6 + 5)
Speed: 0 ft., fly 50 ft. (hover)

STR 1 (-5) | DEX 16 (+3) | CON 12 (+1) | INT 6 (-2) | WIS 12 (+1) | CHA 10 (+0)

**Damage Resistances:** acid, cold, fire, lightning, thunder; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks
**Damage Immunities:** necrotic, poison
**Condition Immunities:** exhaustion, grappled, paralyzed, poisoned, prone, restrained
**Senses:** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 11
**Languages:** —
**Challenge:** 1 (200 XP)

**Incorporeal Movement.** The wisp can move through other creatures and objects as if they were difficult terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.

### Actions

**Life Drain.** *Melee Spell Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 10 (3d6) necrotic damage. The target must succeed on a DC 12 Constitution saving throw or its hit point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to the damage taken.

## Magic Item: Amulet of Twilight

*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)*

This silver amulet is set with a deep violet gem that seems to contain swirling darkness. While attuned to the amulet, you gain the following benefits:

- You have darkvision out to a range of 60 feet. If you already have darkvision, its range increases by 30 feet.
- You can cast *darkness* once per day, requiring no material components.
- While in dim light or darkness, you have advantage on Stealth checks.

**Curse.** Once attuned, the amulet cannot be removed except by a *remove curse* spell. While wearing the amulet, you have vulnerability to radiant damage.

## Magic Item: Shadow Blade

*Weapon (shortsword), uncommon (requires attunement)*

This blade is forged from condensed shadow. It deals an extra 1d6 necrotic damage on a hit. While in dim light or darkness, the blade becomes invisible, giving you advantage on the first attack roll you make each turn.

## Spell: Shadow Step

*3rd-level conjuration*

**Casting Time:** 1 bonus action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You teleport from one shadow to another. You must be in dim light or darkness, and you must be able to see an unoccupied space in dim light or darkness within range. You teleport to that space, and until the end of your turn, you have advantage on the first melee attack you make.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, you can bring one willing creature within 5 feet of you for each slot level above 3rd.
