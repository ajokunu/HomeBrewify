# Relics of the Greymist

## Magic Items for a Dark Fantasy Campaign

*Written by the DM*

---

# Chapter 1: Common and Uncommon Items

## Magic Item: Lantern of Revealing

*Wondrous item, uncommon*

While lit, this hooded lantern burns for 6 hours on 1 pint of oil, shedding bright light in a 30-foot radius and dim light for an additional 30 feet. Invisible creatures and objects are visible as long as they are in the lantern's bright light. You can use an action to lower the hood, reducing the light to dim light in a 5-foot radius.

## Magic Item: Cloak of the Mist

*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)*

While wearing this cloak in fog or mist, you have advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks. While you are in an area of dim light or darkness, you can use your action to become invisible until you move or take an action or a reaction.

## Magic Item: Thornguard Shield

*Armor (shield), uncommon*

While holding this shield, you have a +1 bonus to AC. This bonus is in addition to the shield's normal bonus to AC. When a creature within 5 feet of you hits you with a melee attack, you can use your reaction to deal 2d4 piercing damage to the attacker.

# Chapter 2: Rare Items

## Magic Item: Blade of the Hollow Knight

*Weapon (longsword), rare (requires attunement)*

You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. When you hit an undead creature with this weapon, the undead takes an extra 1d8 radiant damage.

**Undead Bane.** When you hit an undead with this weapon, that creature must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or be turned for 1 minute. A turned creature must spend its turns trying to move as far away from you as it can.

**Curse.** This sword is cursed, and becoming attuned to it extends the curse to you. As long as you remain cursed, you are unwilling to part with the sword, keeping it within reach at all times. While attuned, you have disadvantage on attack rolls made with weapons other than this one.

## Magic Item: Amulet of the Greymist

*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement by a spellcaster)*

This silver amulet is set with a pearl of swirling grey fog. While attuned, you gain the following benefits:

- You can see normally through fog and mist, both magical and nonmagical.
- You can cast *fog cloud* at will, without expending a spell slot.
- Once per long rest, you can cast *gaseous form* on yourself without expending a spell slot.

## Magic Item: Quiver of the Forest Guardian

*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)*

This quiver has 3 charges. While wearing the quiver, you can use a bonus action to expend 1 charge and magically produce one of the following arrows:

- **Bramble Arrow.** On a hit, the target takes an extra 2d6 piercing damage and must succeed on a DC 14 Strength saving throw or become restrained by thorny vines until the end of your next turn.
- **Smoke Arrow.** On a hit, the arrow explodes in a 10-foot-radius sphere of thick smoke centered on the target. The area is heavily obscured for 1 minute or until a strong wind disperses it.
- **Tracking Arrow.** On a hit, you know the direction and distance to the target for the next 24 hours, as long as you are on the same plane.

The quiver regains all expended charges daily at dawn.

# Chapter 3: Very Rare and Legendary Items

## Magic Item: Staff of the Bone Colossus

*Staff, very rare (requires attunement by a warlock or wizard)*

This staff has 10 charges. While holding the staff, you can use an action to expend charges to cast one of the following spells from it, using your spell save DC:

- *animate dead* (3 charges)
- *blight* (4 charges)
- *circle of death* (6 charges)
- *finger of death* (7 charges)

The staff regains 1d6 + 4 expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the staff crumbles to bone dust and is destroyed.

**Bone Shield.** While holding the staff, you can use a reaction when you are hit by an attack to gain a +2 bonus to your AC against that attack, as bones swirl around you to deflect the blow. You can use this property a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, regaining all uses at dawn.

## Magic Item: Crown of the Greymist King

*Wondrous item, legendary (requires attunement)*

This crown of twisted iron and dark gemstones was once worn by the last king of the Greymist realm. While attuned, you gain the following properties:

- Your Charisma score becomes 20 if it is not already 20 or higher.
- You gain resistance to necrotic damage.
- You can cast *dominate person* (save DC 17) once per day. When you do, the target has disadvantage on the saving throw if it is an undead creature.
- You can use an action to cast *circle of power* centered on yourself. Once used, this property can't be used again until the next dawn.

**Sentience.** The Crown of the Greymist King is a sentient neutral evil item with an Intelligence of 16, a Wisdom of 14, and a Charisma of 20. It has hearing and darkvision out to a range of 120 feet. It can communicate telepathically with its wearer.

**Personality.** The crown whispers of dominion and power, urging its wearer to command others and build an army. It seeks a worthy ruler to restore the Greymist Kingdom. If the wearer consistently shows mercy or weakness, a conflict between the crown and its wearer occurs at the next sunset.
