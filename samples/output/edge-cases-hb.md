# Chapter 3: Items with Special Properties

#### Palette of Illusions
*Wondrous item, very rare, (requires attunement by spellcaster)*
:
This painter's palette is carved from petrified wood and set with seven gemstone paints of different colors. While attuned, you gain the following benefits:
- You gain proficiency with painter's supplies if you don't already have it.
- You can cast *minor illusion* at will, and the illusion appears as a painted image floating in the air.
- The palette has **7 charges**. You can use an action to expend charges to cast one of the following spells from it, using your spell save DC:
  - *silent image* (1 charge)
  - *phantasmal force* (2 charges)
  - *major image* (3 charges)
  - *hallucinatory terrain* (4 charges)
  - *programmed illusion* (5 charges)
The palette regains 1d4 + 3 expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the palette's colors fade and it becomes a nonmagical painter's palette.
**Curse.** After creating an illusion with the palette, you must succeed on a DC 12 Wisdom saving throw or become convinced the illusion is real for 1 minute.

#### Mural Fragment
*Wondrous item, rare*
:
This fragment of an ancient mural radiates faint magical energy. You can use an action to press the fragment against a flat surface at least 5 feet wide and 5 feet tall. The fragment creates a lifelike mural on the surface depicting a scene of your choice. The mural is permanent but nonmagical.
If the fragment is pressed against a wall while you speak a command word, the mural becomes a portal to a 10-foot cube extradimensional space for 1 hour. The space is furnished with comfortable chairs and a small table. When the duration ends, any creatures inside the space are expelled to the nearest unoccupied space. This property can be used once per day.
