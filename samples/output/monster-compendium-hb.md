## Greymist Stalker
*Medium monstrosity, neutral*
___
**Armor Class** :: 15
**Hit Points** :: 52 (8d8 + 16)
**Speed** :: 40 ft.
___
|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
|14 (+2)|18 (+4)|14 (+2)|7 (-2)|14 (+2)|8 (-1)|
___
**Saving Throws** :: Dex +6, Wis +4
**Skills** :: Perception +4, Stealth +8
**Damage Resistances** :: cold
**Senses** :: darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 14
**Languages** :: —
___
***Mist Walker.*** The stalker can see normally through fog and mist. It has advantage on Stealth checks made in fog or mist.
:
***Pack Tactics.*** The stalker has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the stalker's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't incapacitated.
:
***Ambush.*** If the stalker surprises a creature and hits it with an attack during the first round of combat, the target takes an extra 7 (2d6) damage from the attack.
:
### Actions {--TOC:exclude}
***Multiattack.*** The stalker makes two claw attacks.
:
***Claw.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) slashing damage.
:
***Mist Leap (Recharge 5-6).*** The stalker teleports up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space it can see that is in dim light or darkness. After teleporting, it can make one claw attack as a bonus action.

{{monster,frame
## Hollow Knight
*Medium undead, lawful evil*
___
**Armor Class** :: 18 (plate armor)
**Hit Points** :: 85 (10d8 + 40)
**Speed** :: 30 ft.
___
|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
|18 (+4)|11 (+0)|18 (+4)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|14 (+2)|
___
**Saving Throws** :: Str +7, Con +7, Wis +4
**Skills** :: Athletics +7, Intimidation +5
**Damage Immunities** :: poison
**Condition Immunities** :: exhaustion, poisoned
**Senses** :: darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 11
**Languages** :: Common
___
***Undead Fortitude.*** If damage reduces the hollow knight to 0 hit points, it must make a Constitution saving throw with a DC of 5 + the damage taken, unless the damage is radiant or from a critical hit. On a success, the hollow knight drops to 1 hit point instead.
:
***Commander's Will.*** As a bonus action, the hollow knight can command one allied undead creature within 30 feet of it to make one weapon attack as a reaction.
:
### Actions {--TOC:exclude}
***Multiattack.*** The hollow knight makes two longsword attacks.
:
***Longsword.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) slashing damage, or 9 (1d10 + 4) slashing damage if used with two hands, plus 4 (1d8) necrotic damage.
:
***Dreadful Command (Recharge 5-6).*** Each creature of the hollow knight's choice that is within 30 feet of the hollow knight and aware of it must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or become frightened for 1 minute. A creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
:
### Reactions {--TOC:exclude}
***Parry.*** The hollow knight adds 3 to its AC against one melee attack that would hit it. To do so, the hollow knight must see the attacker and be wielding a melee weapon.

## Bone Colossus
*Huge undead, chaotic evil*
___
**Armor Class** :: 17 (natural armor)
**Hit Points** :: 161 (14d12 + 70)
**Speed** :: 40 ft.
___
|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
|24 (+7)|8 (-1)|20 (+5)|6 (-2)|10 (+0)|5 (-3)|
___
**Saving Throws** :: Con +9, Wis +4
**Damage Resistances** :: bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks
**Damage Immunities** :: poison, necrotic
**Condition Immunities** :: charmed, exhaustion, frightened, paralyzed, poisoned
**Senses** :: darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 10
**Languages** :: understands Common but can't speak
___
***Siege Monster.*** The colossus deals double damage to objects and structures.
:
***Bone Reconstruction.*** At the start of each of its turns, the colossus regains 10 hit points if it has at least 1 hit point and there are bones within 60 feet.
:
***Immutable Form.*** The colossus is immune to any spell or effect that would alter its form.
:
### Actions {--TOC:exclude}
***Multiattack.*** The bone colossus makes two slam attacks.
:
***Slam.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +11 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 20 (3d8 + 7) bludgeoning damage.
:
***Bone Storm (Recharge 5-6).*** The colossus unleashes a whirlwind of bone shards in a 30-foot cone. Each creature in the area must make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw, taking 36 (8d8) slashing damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one. A creature that fails by 5 or more also has its speed reduced by half until the end of its next turn.
:
### Legendary Actions {--TOC:exclude}
The bone colossus can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The bone colossus regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.
:
***Detect.*** The colossus makes a Wisdom (Perception) check.
:
***Slam (Costs 2 Actions).*** The colossus makes one slam attack.
:
***Bone Surge (Costs 3 Actions).*** Each creature within 15 feet of the colossus must succeed on a DC 17 Strength saving throw or be pushed 10 feet away and knocked prone.

___

**Condition Immunities** :: poisoned

:

:
***Invisibility.*** The imp magically turns invisible until it attacks or until its concentration ends (as if concentrating on a spell).
