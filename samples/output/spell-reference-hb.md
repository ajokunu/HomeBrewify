# Cantrips

#### Chill Mist
*Evocation cantrip*
___
**Casting Time** :: 1 action
**Range** :: 60 feet
**Components** :: V, S
**Duration** :: Instantaneous

You conjure a tendril of freezing mist that lashes out at a creature within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 1d8 cold damage, and its speed is reduced by 10 feet until the start of your next turn.
The spell's damage increases by 1d8 when you reach 5th level (2d8), 11th level (3d8), and 17th level (4d8).

#### Grave Ward
*Abjuration cantrip*
___
**Casting Time** :: 1 action
**Range** :: Touch
**Components** :: V, S
**Duration** :: 1 round

You touch one willing creature and ward it against the undead. Until the start of your next turn, the target has advantage on saving throws against effects from undead creatures, and undead creatures have disadvantage on attack rolls against the target.

# 1st Level Spells

#### Mist Step
*1st-level conjuration*
___
**Casting Time** :: 1 bonus action
**Range** :: Self
**Components** :: V
**Duration** :: Instantaneous

You briefly surround yourself in silvery mist and teleport up to 15 feet to an unoccupied space that you can see.
**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the distance you can teleport increases by 15 feet for each slot level above 1st.

#### Spectral Bindings
*1st-level necromancy*
___
**Casting Time** :: 1 action
**Range** :: 30 feet
**Components** :: V, S, M (a length of chain)
**Duration** :: Concentration, up to 1 minute

Ghostly chains erupt from the ground and wrap around a creature you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be restrained for the duration. An undead creature makes this save with disadvantage.
At the end of each of its turns, the restrained target can make a Strength saving throw, ending the effect on a success. If the target is an undead creature, it takes 1d8 radiant damage at the start of each of its turns while restrained.

# 2nd Level Spells

#### Mistcloak
*2nd-level illusion*
___
**Casting Time** :: 1 action
**Range** :: Self
**Components** :: V, S
**Duration** :: Concentration, up to 10 minutes

You wrap yourself in a cloak of swirling mist. For the duration, you have advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks, and any creature that starts its turn within 5 feet of you must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or be blinded until the start of its next turn. A creature that can see through magical illusions is immune to this blindness.

#### Death's Embrace
*2nd-level necromancy*
___
**Casting Time** :: 1 action
**Range** :: Touch
**Components** :: V, S
**Duration** :: Instantaneous

Make a melee spell attack against a creature you can reach. On a hit, the target takes 3d10 necrotic damage, and you regain hit points equal to half the necrotic damage dealt.
**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d10 for each slot level above 2nd.

# 3rd Level Spells

#### Shadow Step
*3rd-level conjuration*
___
**Casting Time** :: 1 bonus action
**Range** :: 60 feet
**Components** :: V
**Duration** :: Instantaneous

You teleport from one shadow to another. You must be in dim light or darkness, and you must be able to see an unoccupied space in dim light or darkness within range. You teleport to that space, and until the end of your turn, you have advantage on the first melee attack you make.
**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, you can bring one willing creature within 5 feet of you for each slot level above 3rd.

#### Bone Cage
*3rd-level necromancy*
___
**Casting Time** :: 1 action
**Range** :: 60 feet
**Components** :: V, S, M (a knuckle bone)
**Duration** :: Concentration, up to 1 minute

Bones erupt from the ground and form a cage around a point you choose within range. The cage is a 10-foot cube. Any creature inside the cage when it forms must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or be trapped inside.
A trapped creature can use its action to make a Strength check against your spell save DC. On a success, the creature breaks free. The bone cage has AC 15, 30 hit points, and vulnerability to bludgeoning damage. If destroyed, all trapped creatures are freed.
