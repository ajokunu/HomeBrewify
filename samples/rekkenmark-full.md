# The Rekkenmark Time Prison

## A Level 7-9 Eberron Adventure

*A time loop mystery where the academy hides ancient secrets*

---

# Adventure Background

The Academy of Rekkenmark has stood for centuries as Karrnath's premier military institution. But beneath its halls lies something far older—a library catacomb dating back to the Age of Demons, where forbidden knowledge was sealed away by the Gatekeepers.

Three months ago, **Finaeo Vineca**, an elderly wizard and Master Librarian, discovered an artifact in the catacombs: the **Temporal Codex**, a set of 32 spellbooks containing rituals from the plane of Mabar. In attempting to study them, he accidentally triggered a planar breach that began corrupting the surrounding countryside.

Unable to stop the corruption, Finaeo made a desperate choice. He created a temporal loop device, freezing Rekkenmark and the surrounding region in a repeating three-day cycle. His hope was to buy time to find a solution. Instead, he trapped everyone—including himself—in an endless nightmare.

The corruption continues to worsen with each cycle. What began as minor magical disturbances has escalated to demonic incursions and monstrous transformations. The loop is failing, and if it breaks before the Codex is contained, the corruption will spread across all of Karrnath.

---

# Adventure Synopsis

The adventurers arrive in Rekkenmark seeking passage to Stormhome, only to find all travel suspended. They are recruited by the Council of Librarians to investigate "bandits" causing magical chaos in the region. In truth, these bandits are refugees led by **Maknok Deathsong**, an expelled student who discovered the conspiracy and is trying to stop it.

The adventure unfolds over multiple loops:

**Loops 1-2: Discovery.** The party experiences the time loop, witnesses the escalating horror cycle, and begins to understand something is deeply wrong.

**Loops 3-4: Investigation.** The party investigates the bandit camp, learns about Maknok and Finaeo, and discovers the source is within the Academy.

**Loops 5-6: The Academy.** The party infiltrates the Academy, navigates its defenses, and descends into the catacombs.

**Loop 7+: Resolution.** The party confronts the Temporal Codex, either destroying it, containing it, or (if they fail) unleashing the corruption upon Khorvaire.

# Running the Adventure

## The Time Loop Mechanic

The loop resets at 9:00 PM each day, returning everyone to 9:00 AM on Day 1. Only **1-2 party members** (chosen by the DM or randomly) retain memories of previous loops.

### Loop Rules

- **Memory:** Only chosen party members remember. Others have faint deja vu (Wisdom save DC 15 to recall fragments).
- **Items:** Physical possessions reset, except items touching skin during the reset.
- **Death:** Dying triggers an immediate personal reset to the lightning rail at 9:00 AM.
- **NPCs:** No NPCs retain memories across loops.
- **Progress:** Any physical changes to the world (opened doors, defeated enemies) reset.

### The Horror Escalation

Each three-day cycle follows the same pattern:

| Day | Time | Events |
|:---:|:-----|:-------|
| 1 | Morning | Normal activity, faint unease |
| 1 | Afternoon | Minor magical disturbances |
| 1 | Evening | Reports of attacks in outlying villages |
| 2 | Morning | Tension, arguments, paranoia |
| 2 | Afternoon | Fights break out, violence escalates |
| 2 | Evening | Demonic manifestations begin |
| 3 | Morning | Chaos, demonic incursion |
| 3 | Afternoon | Full demonic assault on town |
| 3 | Evening | Catastrophic destruction |

After Day 3, everything resets to Day 1.

The party might arrive in Rekkenmark for various reasons:

- **Passage to Stormhome:** Seeking transport to the island nation of Aundair.
- **Academy Business:** Delivering a message or package to the Academy.
- **Investigation:** Hired by House Jorasco or Cannith to investigate magical disturbances.
- **Personal:** One character has ties to the Academy or Rekkenmark.

# Chapter 1: Arrival

## Rekkenmark Station

> The lightning rail hisses to a stop, steam billowing across the platform. Rekkenmark rises before you—a city of iron spires and grey stone, perched on the banks of the Cyre River. Soldiers in the black and crimson of Karrnath patrol the platform, their movements precise and watchful. The morning air carries the smell of the river, coal smoke, and something else—a faint ozone tang that prickles your skin.

**Rekkenmark** is a city of 12,000 souls, dominated by the Academy of Rekkenmark—the premier military training institution in Khorvaire. Since the Treaty of Thronehold, the city has struggled to find purpose. Many soldiers have become guards or mercenaries. The Academy now trains more scholars than soldiers.

### Key Locations at the Station

**The Platform.** Wide stone platform with iron railings. 2d4 Karrnathi soldiers stand guard at any time. A brass clock tower shows the time prominently.

**The Ticket Office.** A small stone building where **Clerk Tomas** (commoner) sells tickets and provides information. He knows:
- All ships to Stormhome have been suspended "by order of the Librarians"
- Strange things have been happening in the countryside
- The Academy is accepting visitors seeking audience with the Council

**The Luggage House.** Where travelers store belongings. **Baggage Master Vornn** (veteran) is a retired soldier, gruff but helpful.

---

## The City of Rekkenmark

### General Features

The city is built on terraced levels rising from the river. The Academy crowns the highest terrace.

**Streets.** Cobblestone, well-maintained but showing age. Wide enough for military formations.

**Buildings.** Grey stone with iron fittings. Many display military honors or family crests.

**Atmosphere.** On Day 1, orderly and calm. On Day 2, tense and hostile. On Day 3, pure chaos.

### Notable Locations

#### The Docks

> Wooden piers stretch into the grey waters of the Cyre River. Storm galleys bob at anchor, their elemental rings dormant. Dock workers sit idle, playing cards and grumbling about lost wages.

**Harbormaster Kella** (noble) is frustrated by the suspension. She knows:
- The Librarians gave no explanation for the suspension
- Ships have been grounded for "at least a week" (she thinks)
- Strange lights have been seen over the Academy at night

**Storm Galley Crews** are bored and irritable. A DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) check gets them talking about:
- Screams heard from villages upriver
- "Bandits" that fight like madmen
- A young orc wizard blamed for the troubles

#### The Soldier's Rest Inn

> A sturdy three-story building of grey stone, its sign depicting a sleeping soldier beneath crossed swords. The common room smells of pipe smoke and mutton stew. Veterans of the Last War nurse their drinks at scarred wooden tables.

**Innkeeper Morra Blackwood** (veteran) is a retired Academy instructor. She is perceptive and suspicious of strangers. Room rates: 5 sp/night for common, 2 gp/night for private.

Morra knows:
- Something is wrong at the Academy—she's seen her former colleagues acting strangely
- The "bandits" started appearing three months ago
- Finaeo Vineca hasn't been seen publicly in weeks

#### The Market Square

A central plaza where merchants sell goods. On Day 1, busy and normal. On Day 2, arguments and fights. On Day 3, abandoned or scene of violence.

**Notable Merchants:**
- **Weapons:** Gorn's Steel (blacksmith, sells standard weapons)
- **Supplies:** Trader Mika's (general goods)
- **Potions:** Sister Helene's (priestess of the Sovereign Host, sells healing potions at standard prices)

## The Academy of Rekkenmark

### Exterior

> The Academy dominates the highest terrace of Rekkenmark—a fortress of grey stone and iron spires. Its walls are thirty feet high, patrolled by robed figures whose hands crackle with arcane energy. Above the main gate, the Academy's motto is carved in stone: "Knowledge Through Discipline."

**The Gate.** Heavy iron doors, always guarded by 2 **mages** (use the mage stat block). They permit entry only to those with "official business."

Getting In:
- **Legitimate:** Request audience with the Council of Librarians (they are summoned within an hour)
- **Deception:** DC 15 Charisma (Deception) check to pose as messengers or officials
- **Stealth:** DC 18 Dexterity (Stealth) to slip past during guard change (6 AM and 6 PM)
- **Force:** Not recommended—the Academy has 20+ mages on staff

### The Council of Librarians

If the party seeks audience, they are escorted to the Grand Library.

> The Grand Library takes your breath away. Seven stories of books rise around you, connected by spiraling staircases and floating platforms. At the center, a semicircular desk of polished mahogany, behind which sit five robed figures. Their faces are lined with worry, their eyes shadowed with exhaustion.

The Council consists of five Master Librarians:

| Name | Role | Personality |
|:-----|:-----|:------------|
| **Archmage Thessa Vol** | Head Librarian | Imperious, hides fear behind authority |
| **Master Korvin** | Military History | Blunt, suspicious of strangers |
| **Mistress Yenna** | Arcane Studies | Nervous, knows more than she admits |
| **Master Dorian** | Natural Philosophy | Elderly, confused about recent events |
| **Master Senna** | Cultural Archives | Young, recently appointed, afraid |

#### What the Council Says

> "Thank you for coming. Rekkenmark faces a grave threat. Bandits—led by a former student named Maknok Deathsong—have been attacking villages in our jurisdiction. They wield dangerous magic that... destabilizes reality. We need capable individuals to track them down and end this threat."

**Offered Reward:** 500 gp each, plus free passage to Stormhome.

**What They're Hiding:**
- Finaeo Vineca is the true source of the problems
- They know about the time loop (they reset too, but Thessa has developed a ritual to retain fragments of memory)
- They're desperate to maintain control and avoid outside intervention

**DC 15 Wisdom (Insight)** reveals they're hiding something. **DC 20** reveals they're terrified of something inside the Academy itself.

## The Bandit Camp

Following the trail leads to the bandit camp after 2 hours of travel.

### Approach

> The trail leads into dense forest, following a stream up into the hills. As evening approaches, you smell wood smoke ahead. Through the trees, you glimpse tents and lean-tos clustered in a rocky hollow. Figures move among them—some walking, some rocking back and forth, some lying motionless.

**Stealth Approach:** DC 13 Dexterity (Stealth) to approach undetected.

**Open Approach:** The "bandits" are too broken to mount effective resistance. They call out warnings but don't attack unless provoked.

### The Camp

> This is not a military encampment. It is a refuge of the damned. Fifty-odd people huddle around dying fires, their eyes hollow, their hands trembling. Some mutter prayers. Others stare at nothing. Many bear the marks of transformation—extra fingers, patches of scales, eyes that have multiplied. A young orc in tattered wizard's robes approaches, hands raised in peace.

**Camp Population:**
- 12 former farmers
- 8 former soldiers (deserters)
- 15 family members (women, children, elderly)
- 10 partially transformed individuals
- 5 fully transformed (twisted bandits, restrained)

## NPC: Maknok Deathsong

*The Accused Innocent*

> The orc before you is barely an adult—perhaps eighteen years old. His green skin is marked with arcane tattoos that glow faintly in the dimming light. His yellow eyes are bloodshot, his hands shaking. When he speaks, his voice cracks with exhaustion.
>
> "Please. We're not your enemy. We're trying to stop this. Let me explain."

**Background:** Maknok was a prodigy at the Academy, youngest student ever admitted. Three months ago, he discovered Finaeo Vineca conducting strange rituals in the catacombs. When he reported this, Finaeo had him expelled and blamed for the magical disturbances.

**Personality:** Brilliant but young. Terrified but determined. Speaks quickly, often forgets others can't follow his magical theorizing.

**What Maknok Knows:**
- Finaeo found something called "the Codex" in the catacombs
- The magic is from Mabar—the plane of endless night
- He believes the only way to stop it is to enter the catacombs and destroy the Codex
- He's been trying to raid the Academy for weeks, but the time loop keeps resetting his progress

**Roleplaying Maknok:** Nervous hand gestures. References complex magical theory. Alternates between hope (finally, someone who might help!) and despair (we're all doomed).

### Maknok's Proposal

> "I know a way into the catacombs—a maintenance tunnel that bypasses most of the Academy. But I can't do this alone. I've tried. Every time I get close, I die, and it all resets. Please. Help me end this."

If the party agrees to help, Maknok provides:
- A rough map of the Academy
- The location of the maintenance tunnel
- What he knows about the catacombs (incomplete)

# Chapter 4: The Catacombs

## Overview

The catacombs extend three levels beneath the Academy. Originally Dhakaani goblin tombs, they were repurposed by early Karrnathi scholars to store forbidden knowledge. Finaeo Vineca discovered the Temporal Codex here.

### General Features

**Lighting:** Dim light from phosphorescent fungi. Magical darkness in some areas.

**Ceilings:** 10 feet high in corridors, 20 feet in chambers.

**Walls:** Ancient stone, carved with goblin pictographs and later Karrnathi inscriptions.

**Air:** Musty, with an underlying smell of rot and ozone.

**Hazard:** The Mabaran corruption creates the following effects:
- Long rests impossible (nightmares prevent benefit)
- Healing magic is halved
- Undead have advantage on saving throws

---

**Creatures:** 2 **shadows** lurk in the darkness, attacking any who carry light sources.

**Features:**
- Four corridors lead to C2, C3, C4, and C5

### C2. Scholar's Alcove

> A small chamber converted into a study. A desk and chair, now rotted, hold the remains of ancient research. Shelves along the walls hold crumbling scrolls.

**Treasure:**

> This corridor has partially collapsed, rubble blocking the way forward. A narrow gap remains at the top.

**Navigation:** DC 14 Acrobatics to squeeze through, or clear rubble (1 hour, makes noise).

**Beyond:** Leads to C7 on Level 2.

### C4. Burial Niches

> A long corridor lined with burial niches, each containing the bones of ancient scholars. Some niches have been disturbed, their contents scattered.

**Creatures:** 3 **skeletons** animate if the corridor is traversed.

**Treasure:** DC 15 Investigation in the niches finds a **potion of healing** and 45 gp in ancient coins.

### C5. The First Fell Bookcase

> This chamber holds a massive bookcase of black stone, carved with symbols that hurt to look at. Several of its shelves are empty, but eight books remain—bound in what might be leather, their covers blank.

**The Bookcase:** One of four "Fell Bookcases" that hold the Temporal Codex. Each bookcase should hold 8 books, spelling out one letter of "R.A.S.K." when complete.

**The Books:** These are Codex books. Touching one requires a DC 13 Wisdom save or take 2d6 psychic damage and see visions of Mabar.

**Puzzle:** The books must be arranged in a specific order. Clues are found throughout the catacombs.

## Level 3: The Codex Chamber

### C9. The Hall of Books

> The corridor opens into a vast circular chamber. The walls are lined with empty shelves, and in the center, four bookcases of black stone stand facing inward, forming a square. Between them, a stone pedestal waits. The floor is inscribed with hundreds of arcane symbols, some glowing faintly.

**The Puzzle:** All 32 Codex books must be placed in the correct order on the four bookcases. The books are scattered throughout the catacombs and Academy.

**Book Locations:**
- Fell Bookcase 1 (C5): 8 books present
- Fell Bookcase 2 (C10): 6 books present, 2 missing
- Fell Bookcase 3 (C11): 5 books present, 3 missing
- Fell Bookcase 4 (C12): 7 books present, 1 missing
- Missing books: 6 scattered in the Academy, held by NPCs, or in player possession

**The Order:** When correctly arranged, the spines spell R-A-S-K (Maknok can help decipher this—it's an ancient Goblin code).

### C10-C12. Secondary Fell Bookcases

These chambers each contain one Fell Bookcase, similar to C5. Each is guarded:
- **C10:** 2 **specters**
- **C11:** 1 **wight** and 3 **zombies**
- **C12:** 3 **shadows**

### The Final Confrontation

When the books are arranged correctly, the pedestal activates.

> The floor trembles. The symbols blaze with cold light. From the pedestal, a column of shadow rises, coalescing into a face—ancient, wizened, filled with malevolent intelligence. The face of an old man, but twisted with a madness that spans centuries.

This is **Vos Nosu**, the entity that created the Codex millennia ago. His consciousness was bound into the books, waiting for someone to complete the ritual and free him.

**Damage Immunities** cold, necrotic, poison
**Condition Immunities** charmed, exhaustion, frightened, grappled, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, prone, restrained
**Senses** truesight 120 ft., passive Perception 12
**Languages** all, telepathy 120 ft.
**Challenge** 12 (8,400 XP)

***Incorporeal Movement.*** Vos Nosu can move through other creatures and objects as if they were difficult terrain. He takes 5 (1d10) force damage if he ends his turn inside an object.

***Legendary Resistance (3/Day).*** If Vos Nosu fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.

***Magic Resistance.*** Vos Nosu has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

***Actions***

***Multiattack.*** Vos Nosu uses Eye Lightning twice.

***Eye Lightning.*** *Ranged Spell Attack:* +9 to hit, range 120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 28 (8d6) lightning damage.

***Summon Demons (Recharge 5-6).*** Vos Nosu summons 1d4+1 **dretches** or 1 **shadow demon** that appear in unoccupied spaces within 60 feet. The demons act on their own initiative and obey Vos Nosu's commands.

***Spellcasting.*** Vos Nosu casts one of the following spells, requiring no components (spell save DC 17):
- At will: *mage hand*, *minor illusion*
- 3/day each: *counterspell*, *dispel magic*, *hold person*
- 1/day each: *banishment*, *circle of death*

***Legendary Actions***

Vos Nosu can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. Vos Nosu regains spent legendary actions at the start of his turn.

**Eye Bolt.** Vos Nosu makes one Eye Lightning attack.
**Fade (Costs 2 Actions).** Vos Nosu becomes invisible until the start of his next turn or until he attacks.
**Maddening Whispers (Costs 2 Actions).** Each creature within 30 feet must succeed on a DC 17 Wisdom saving throw or take 14 (4d6) psychic damage and be stunned until the end of its next turn.

# Conclusion

## Victory

If the party defeats Vos Nosu and contains or destroys the Codex:

> The face of Vos Nosu screams silently as it dissolves into motes of shadow. The pedestal crumbles. Around you, the catacombs shudder—and somewhere above, a clock strikes ten. A time that should be impossible. The loop is broken.
>
> You emerge from the Academy into morning light—true morning light, not the endless repeat. Rekkenmark is damaged but alive. The corruption is fading. Ships are preparing to sail. Maknok Deathsong, cleared of all charges, weeps with relief.

**Rewards:**
- 500 gp each from the Council (doubled if Finaeo survives)
- Free passage to anywhere in Khorvaire
- Reputation: +2 to Karrnathi faction standing
- Potential patron: House Cannith or House Jorasco may want to study the remains of the Codex

## Partial Victory

If the party destroys the Codex but the loop breaks unexpectedly:

> The corruption that was building breaks free in one catastrophic moment. The ritual circle explodes outward. Demons pour through cracks in reality. Rekkenmark burns. You fight your way out, but behind you, thousands die. The Last War's end may not hold after all.

The party survives but carries the guilt. Karrnath mobilizes for war. The campaign continues with grave consequences.

## Failure

If the party fails to stop Vos Nosu:

> Vos Nosu's laughter echoes through the catacombs as his power swells. The loop shatters. Portals to Mabar tear open across Karrnath. The plane of endless night spills into the world. Rekkenmark is the first city to fall, but it will not be the last.

The campaign takes a dark turn. The party must rally Khorvaire against a planar invasion.

# Appendix D: Adjusting Difficulty

## Scaling for Levels 5-6

- Reduce Vos Nosu to CR 10 (remove Legendary Actions)
- Reduce Finaeo to CR 7 (remove 6th level spell slot)
- Replace shadow demons with shadows
- Reduce twisted bandit HP to 30

## Scaling for Levels 9-10

- Increase Vos Nosu to CR 14 (add lair actions)
- Increase number of demons summoned
- Add a second wraith to C6
- Include additional Codex fragments as rewards

## Adding Challenge

- The Codex books move between loops (party must find them again)
- Only one party member remembers each loop
- Time limit: The loop fails after 10 cycles regardless

## Reducing Challenge

- Maknok can join as an NPC ally
- Finaeo can be reasoned with without a check
- Provide more healing potions throughout
- Allow long rests in the refugee camp
