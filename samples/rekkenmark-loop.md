# The Rekkenmark Recursion

## A Level 6-7 Eberron Adventure

*A twisted crime thriller where death is only the beginning*

---

# Chapter 1: The First Death

## Location: Rekkenmark, Karrnath

The iron spires of Rekkenmark pierce a perpetually grey sky. This city of soldiers and secrets sits along the Cyre River, its famous military academy casting long shadows over cobblestone streets still stained with the memory of war. Two years after the Treaty of Thronehold, tension simmers beneath the veneer of peace.

> You arrive at Rekkenmark Station as the lightning rail hisses to a stop. The platform is nearly empty save for a few Karrnathi soldiers in their distinctive black and red uniforms. A cold wind carries the smell of the river and something else—burnt ozone and copper. The station clock reads 6:47 PM. Remember that time.

### The Murder

The party has been hired by **Baroness Ylara ir'Tannath** to investigate threats against her family. Within an hour of arriving, they witness a brutal murder in the **Ossuary District**—a Karrnathi noble named **Lord Vedrim Halar** is killed by a figure in a silver mask. Before dying, Vedrim gasps: *"The Recursion... it's already begun..."*

Then everything goes white.

---

# Chapter 2: Yesterday, Again

> Your eyes snap open. You're on the lightning rail, pulling into Rekkenmark Station. The clock reads 6:47 PM. The same cold wind. The same smell. The same soldiers on the platform. But you remember dying—or do you?

The players are trapped in a **temporal loop** caused by an artifact called the **Hourglass of Mabar**, stolen from a Jorasco research facility. Every time they die—or when the clock strikes midnight—time resets to 6:47 PM.

### The Rules of the Loop

**DM Note:** Track loop iterations. Each loop:
- Resets to 6:47 PM at Rekkenmark Station
- Players retain memories and knowledge
- Physical items reset (except items touching skin during reset)
- NPCs have no memory of previous loops
- The loop breaks only when the crime is truly solved

## Key Locations

## Location: The Ossuary District

A neighborhood built atop ancient Karrnathi catacombs. Bone motifs decorate every building. The streets twist like a maze, and the locals speak in whispers. Lord Vedrim's murder occurs here at 7:42 PM in the **Marrowgate Plaza**.

### Marrowgate Plaza

> Pale streetlamps cast sickly light across flagstones inlaid with skeletal patterns. A fountain shaped like clasped skeletal hands stands at the center, water trickling from between bony fingers. The plaza is quiet—too quiet for this hour.

## Location: Rekkenmark Academy

The famous military academy looms over the city. Its walls have trained the greatest generals in Khorvaire's history. Currently hosting a **secret summit** between Karrnathi military officials and a Brelish delegation—a summit that someone wants to fail.

## Location: The Crimson Quill Inn

A respectable establishment near the academy. The party's base of operations. The innkeeper, **Morra Blackwood**, knows more than she lets on.

> The inn's common room smells of mulled wine and wood smoke. Mounted military honors line the walls, and a crackling fire struggles against the Karrnathi chill. A woman with steel-grey hair polishes glasses behind the bar, her eyes tracking every patron with trained precision.

# Chapter 3: The Conspiracy

## The Truth (DM Only)

**Note:** The murder is not what it seems. Lord Vedrim Halar is actually the mastermind of a plot to assassinate the Brelish delegation and reignite the Last War. The "murder" the party witnesses is staged—Vedrim faked his death to create an alibi while his agents carry out the real attack at midnight.

The silver-masked killer is actually **Lady Sera Halar**, Vedrim's sister, who discovered his plot and is trying to stop him. She killed his body double, thinking it was him.

The Hourglass of Mabar was activated accidentally when Lady Sera tried to use it to gather evidence. Now everyone in a three-block radius is caught in the loop—including Vedrim, who doesn't know the loop is happening.

### The Real Crime

At midnight, Vedrim's agents—three **Emerald Claw operatives**—will poison the Brelish delegation at the Academy. Unless the party stops them, twelve diplomats die, and the fragile peace shatters.

---

## Key NPCs

## NPC: Lord Vedrim Halar

*The "Victim" Who Is Actually the Villain*

A decorated Karrnathi war hero with a prosthetic arm made of darkwood and adamantine. Publicly, he advocates for peace. Privately, he believes Karrnath was robbed of victory and plots to restart the war.

**Roleplaying Vedrim:** Charming, patriotic, dismissive of "foreign weakness." He genuinely believes he's saving his nation.

## NPC: Lady Sera Halar

*The Masked Killer Who Is Actually the Hero*

Vedrim's younger sister, a spy for the King's Dark Lanterns (Breland's intelligence service). She discovered her brother's plot and has been trying to stop him for months. Wears the silver mask to hide her identity.

**Roleplaying Sera:** Desperate, paranoid, reluctant to trust. Will initially flee from the party but can become an ally.

## NPC: Morra Blackwood

*The Innkeeper With Secrets*

Former Rekkenmark Academy instructor, now retired. Knows about the secret summit and suspects something is wrong. Has a network of former students throughout the city.

**Roleplaying Morra:** Gruff, observant, speaks in military metaphors. Fiercely protective of "her city."

# Chapter 4: Loop Progression

## Loop 1: Confusion

The party arrives, witnesses the murder, likely dies or reaches midnight without understanding what's happening.

## Loop 2-3: Investigation

Players begin to piece together the timeline. Key discoveries:
- The "victim" was seen alive after his death
- The silver-masked figure is female
- Something is happening at the Academy at midnight

## Loop 4-5: Confrontation

Players can confront Lady Sera, learn the truth about Vedrim, and begin planning to stop the real attack.

## Loop 6+: Resolution

Players must:
1. Prevent the assassination at the Academy
2. Expose or eliminate Vedrim
3. Destroy or contain the Hourglass of Mabar

## Encounters

### Monster: Emerald Claw Operative

*Medium humanoid (human), lawful evil*

AC: 16 (breastplate)
HP: 45 (7d8+14)
Speed: 30 ft.

STR 14 DEX 16 CON 14 INT 12 WIS 11 CHA 10

**Sneak Attack (1/turn).** The operative deals an extra 14 (4d6) damage when hitting a target with advantage or when an ally is within 5 feet of the target.

**Fanatical Devotion.** The operative has advantage on saving throws against being frightened or charmed.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The operative makes two shortsword attacks.

**Shortsword.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d6+3) piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) poison damage.

**Poisoned Dart.** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, range 20/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d4+3) piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) poison damage. DC 14 Constitution save or poisoned for 1 minute.

---

### Monster: Vedrim's Body Double

*Medium undead, neutral evil*

AC: 14 (natural armor)
HP: 52 (8d8+16)
Speed: 30 ft.

STR 16 DEX 12 CON 14 INT 8 WIS 10 CHA 6

**Undead Fortitude.** If damage reduces the body double to 0 hit points, it must make a Constitution saving throw with a DC of 5 + the damage taken, unless the damage is radiant or from a critical hit. On a success, the body double drops to 1 hit point instead.

**False Appearance.** While motionless, the body double is indistinguishable from Lord Vedrim.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The body double makes two slam attacks.

**Slam.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 10 (2d6+3) bludgeoning damage.

---

### Monster: Lord Vedrim Halar

*Medium humanoid (human), lawful evil*

AC: 18 (plate armor)
HP: 85 (10d8+40)
Speed: 30 ft.

STR 18 DEX 12 CON 18 INT 14 WIS 13 CHA 16

**War Caster.** Vedrim has advantage on Constitution saving throws to maintain concentration. He can perform somatic components with weapons in hand.

**Adamantine Arm.** Vedrim's prosthetic arm grants him advantage on Strength checks and his unarmed strikes deal 1d8+4 bludgeoning damage.

**Legendary Resistance (2/Day).** If Vedrim fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** Vedrim makes three attacks with his longsword or two attacks and casts a spell.

**Longsword.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d8+4) slashing damage, or 9 (1d10+4) slashing damage if used with two hands.

**Adamantine Fist.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d8+4) bludgeoning damage. On a critical hit, the target must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution save or be stunned until the end of its next turn.

**Spellcasting.** Vedrim casts one of the following spells (spell save DC 14):
- At will: *thaumaturgy*, *spare the dying*
- 2/day each: *hold person*, *spiritual weapon*, *command*
- 1/day: *spirit guardians*

## Magic Item: Hourglass of Mabar

*Wondrous item, legendary (requires attunement)*

This palm-sized hourglass contains black sand that seems to absorb light. Created by House Jorasco researchers studying the plane of Mabar, it was intended to "pause" death for dying patients.

When shattered or activated incorrectly, the Hourglass creates a temporal loop affecting all creatures within 500 feet. The loop resets at midnight or when the attuned creature dies.

**Breaking the Loop.** The loop can be broken by:
- Reuniting the Hourglass's two halves (if shattered)
- Casting *dispel magic* at 7th level or higher on the Hourglass
- Killing the creature who activated it while they hold the Hourglass
- Completing the "unfinished business" that triggered the loop

**Curse.** A creature attuned to the Hourglass cannot die permanently while the loop is active. They always reset.

# Chapter 5: Climax

## The Final Loop

To break the loop, the party must:

1. **Stop the Assassination (Before Midnight)**
   - Infiltrate the Academy during the summit
   - Identify and neutralize the three Emerald Claw operatives
   - Prevent the poison from reaching the delegates

2. **Confront Lord Vedrim**
   - He will be at his estate, preparing his alibi
   - Combat encounter or social confrontation
   - Must be killed or captured while Lady Sera holds the Hourglass

3. **Contain the Hourglass**
   - Lady Sera must willingly relinquish it
   - It can be given to House Jorasco, House Cannith, or destroyed

> The clock tower strikes midnight. But this time, instead of the world going white, you hear it—the second chime. Then the third. Time moves forward. Lady Sera lowers the Hourglass, its black sand finally still. "It's over," she whispers. "It's finally over."

# Appendix: Rewards

## Magic Item: Sera's Silver Mask

*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)*

This elegant silver mask covers the upper face. While wearing it:

- You have advantage on Charisma (Deception) checks
- You can cast *disguise self* at will
- Once per long rest, you can cast *nondetection* on yourself

**Curse.** While attuned, you have disadvantage on Wisdom (Insight) checks against creatures you consider allies.

## Conclusion

The Rekkenmark Recursion can end several ways:

**Best Outcome:** The assassination is stopped, Vedrim is exposed, Lady Sera is vindicated, and the peace holds. The party gains powerful allies in Breland's intelligence service.

**Partial Success:** The assassination is stopped, but Vedrim escapes. He becomes a recurring villain, and the party is now on the Emerald Claw's radar.

**Failure:** If the loop is broken without stopping the assassination, the delegates die, and the Last War threatens to reignite. The party must live with their failure—and the knowledge that they could have prevented it.

> The grey skies of Rekkenmark seem lighter as you board the lightning rail departing the city. The clock tower reads 6:47 AM—twelve hours after you first arrived, though it feels like a lifetime. Some loops, it seems, are meant to be broken.
