# The Rekkenmark Time Prison

## A Mid-Level Eberron Adventure

*A time loop mystery where the academy hides ancient secrets*

---

# Act 1: Arrival and Entrapment

## Location: Rekkenmark Station

The group arrives off the lightning rail in the town of Rekkenmark. The group likely came here to get passage to Stormhome via a storm galley.

> When they arrive via the Lightning rail, all seems well. The morning air is crisp, the platform bustling with merchants and soldiers. Rekkenmark's famous spires rise against a grey Karrnathi sky.

When the group attempts to book passage on a Storm Galley to Stormhome, however, the boats tell them that they are not setting sail as the Librarians have decreed that all transports are halting pending an investigation.

## Location: The Grand Library

Should the adventurers seek an audience for passage, they are directed to the Grand Library in the Academy to meet with the Council of Librarians who run the Academy of Rekkenmark.

> The Grand Library stretches before you, its vaulted ceilings disappearing into shadow. Thousands of books line walls that seem to go on forever. At the center, a semicircular desk where robed figures wait with expressions of barely concealed desperation.

The librarians beseech them to track down bandits that have been murdering and attacking towns in the nearby area. The librarians claim these bandits are using some sort of magic to ruin the lives of townsfolk and cause random magic to happen all over the region.

---

## The Time Loop Trigger

**Note:** If the group tries to leave when the clock strikes 9 PM wherever they are, they are teleported back again as if they had never left. Some strange power keeps them locked here.

If they ask the Librarians, they say the bandits are responsible, and it is the work of **Maknok Deathsong**, an 18-year-old male orc wizard who is an ex-student prodigy of the academy.

{{note
##### Important DM Rule

When the day resets, you must choose 1 or 2 members of your party to remember the previous day's events. No one else will be able to remember them.

This creates tension and roleplay opportunities as players must convince their own party members of the truth each loop.
}}

---

# The Three-Day Cycle of Horror

## Day 1: Normalcy

Everything seems reasonable. The town functions normally. People go about their business.

## Day 2: Tension

> The world is even more horrible. The dockworkers and people are angry and red-faced at all times. People are angsty and ready to fight. Arguments break out over nothing. Violence simmers beneath every interaction.

The Council of Librarians seems unaffected and gives them the same mission.

## Day 3: Chaos

> A hole opens in the middle of town. Demons pour out. Fights rage through the streets—stabbings, murder, chaos everywhere. A horrible pit of filth and debauchery consumes the city.

Should the group attempt to escape, they make it to the edge of the zone by nightfall only to awake on the third day back on the lightning rail with the world in utter chaos.

## Day 4: Reset

The town resets to that of the first day they arrived. Everything seems reasonable. The 3-day cycle of horror continues.

# Act 1 Encounters

## Location: The Attacked Village

The adventurers venture forth to defend the next town from the attack that the librarians say is within the bandits' magic influence.

> You find a horrific scene. The bandits seem feral and twisted as if affected by evil magic that takes no care for their lives. Bodies lie broken and dead or deformed. Some bandits are maimed. Others are halfway through a transformation into aberrant beasts—just dying or tearing apart, their bodies destroyed.

## Location: The Bandit Camp

If the group follows them to their camp, they see men rocking back and forth, seemingly mad.

> The camp is less a military encampment and more a refuge of the damned. Figures huddle around dying fires, their eyes hollow, their hands shaking. Some mutter words that might once have been prayers.

## NPC: Maknok Deathsong

*The Accused Who May Be Innocent*

An 18-year-old male orc wizard, former prodigy of the Academy. If the players gain his trust, they will find out that he is telling the truth and doesn't know what's going on.

**What Maknok Knows:**
- He senses strange energies radiating from the Academy
- He plans to sneak into the academy to investigate
- He discovered something in the library catacombs before the loops began

**Roleplaying Maknok:** Young, brilliant, terrified. He speaks quickly, constantly looking over his shoulder. His hands never stop moving—a nervous habit from years of spellcasting.

---

## The Child Encounter

> Upon leaving the camp, the party stumbles over the dead body of a child lying in the grass. Newly deceased, still warm.

Should they decide to take him with them back to his family, he deforms in the middle of the night into a horrible creature—his body ripped apart and reformed.

### Monster: Corrupted Child

*Large aberration, chaotic evil*

**Use Gibbering Mouther statistics, buffed for larger parties:**

AC: 9
HP: 95 (10d10+40)
Speed: 10 ft., swim 10 ft.

STR 12 DEX 8 CON 18 INT 3 WIS 10 CHA 6

**Aberrant Ground.** The ground in a 10-foot radius around the mouther is doughlike difficult terrain. Each creature that starts its turn in that area must succeed on a DC 12 Strength saving throw or have its speed reduced to 0 until the start of its next turn.

**Gibbering.** The mouther babbles incoherently while it can see any creature and isn't incapacitated. Each creature that starts its turn within 20 feet of the mouther and can hear the gibbering must succeed on a DC 12 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, the creature can't take reactions until the start of its next turn and rolls a d8 to determine what it does during its turn.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The mouther makes two bite attacks.

**Bites.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 17 (5d6) piercing damage. If the target is Medium or smaller, it must succeed on a DC 12 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

**Blinding Spittle (Recharge 5-6).** The mouther spits a chemical glob at a point it can see within 15 feet of it. The glob explodes in a blinding flash of light on impact. Each creature within 5 feet of the flash must succeed on a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw or be blinded until the end of the mouther's next turn.

# Act 2: Searching the Academy

## Location: The Academy Grounds

When they attempt to search the academy, the place is under heavy watch by crazed wizards—actually students and teachers tasked with protecting the academy from intruders.

> The Academy's corridors echo with whispered incantations. Robed figures patrol with wild eyes and crackling fingertips. These are not guards—they are zealots, defending secrets they may not even understand.

## The True Villain

## NPC: Finaeo Vineca

*Master Wizard and Architect of the Loop*

A master wizard of the highest order, unable to seemingly stop the bandits and horrible things happening around them, he bound this magically torn countryside in a time loop powered by a curious device he created in a personal laboratory.

> Finaeo Vineca stands before you—ancient beyond reckoning. Half his body is consumed by a massive Dragonmark of Making, glowing with barely contained power. A prodigy of his House grown old, left to teach others while his library spreads deep beneath the academy.

**What Finaeo Knows:**
- He created the time loop to contain the magical chaos
- The source is something deep in the catacombs
- He has lost control of his own creation

---

# Act 3: The Catacombs

## Location: The Library Catacombs

The players descend into ancient tunnels beneath the Academy, where the true source of the chaos awaits.

## The Truth About Maknok

If the players return to the bandit mage in the woods, they meet what appears to be an old adventuring group, frayed at the edges and wild.

**What Deathsong Reveals:**
- They are not bandits—they are refugees going mad watching their brethren turned and killed
- They planned to storm the academy, believing the mages created this magic storm
- Before they could attack, the time loop began
- During one investigation, Deathsong discovered a strange spellbook deep in the library

---

## The Codex Puzzle

### Magic Item: The First Spellbook of the Codex

*Wondrous item, legendary*

The spells are written in an old High Elvish language in some sort of system. When written back out, they seem to establish a kind of ritual Elvish spell, except there are dozens of them on each page.

Each book gives off a faint magical aura that can be felt when holding it—similar to the 4 "Fell" bookcases in the center catacomb below the academy.

{{note
##### The Puzzle

There are **30 books** strewn about the Catacombs Library. Each must be found and placed back in the original order on the wall-carved bookcases in the center of the library catacombs.

When placed correctly, the books spell out **R.A.S.K.**

However, there are **32 books in total**. One was taken for the mage's brain source code. One remains missing.
}}

## The Pedestal Puzzle

When the books have been placed in their perspective places, a small plateau raises from the ground. Four separate clock pieces appear with 8 switches on each.

> Inscribed in the middle of the stone: "Take the middle and enforce it to your mettle. The center will allow you to enter."

---

# Act 4: The Finale

## The Revelation

When the keys are placed correctly, an image of a face appears above the pedestal.

> The face in the image is a much older version of R.A.S.K., wizened with age. He speaks with a voice that echoes from decades past—or perhaps decades yet to come.

**The Truth:**
- The figure created this Source Code for his brain using many spells and rituals
- Once put back together, his consciousness was restored
- He hoped to go back in time and transfer his brain into a warforged to live out his eternal purpose

## The Campaign Connection

{{note
##### Campaign-Specific: Vos Nosu

In my campaign, this person is actually **Vos Nosu**, brother of Sak Nosu. After being tricked into killing his brother by the player characters and House Jorasco, he went insane and took on the personality of R.A.S.K.

He studied magic for 100 years, finally using the Magister to create a time-travel device. Failing in health and age, the device brought him too far back. With no way to fix it, he imbued his consciousness into these 32 books using his own magic source code.
}}

---

## Final Encounter

The semi-conscious version of himself attacks with a lightning bolt from his eyes, and a fight breaks out.

### Monster: Vos Nosu, The Fractured Mind

*Medium undead (spirit), chaotic evil*

AC: 15 (natural armor)
HP: 136 (16d8+64)
Speed: 0 ft., fly 40 ft. (hover)

STR 6 DEX 16 CON 18 INT 20 WIS 14 CHA 16

**Damage Resistances** acid, fire, lightning, thunder; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks
**Damage Immunities** cold, necrotic, poison
**Condition Immunities** charmed, exhaustion, frightened, grappled, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, prone, restrained

**Incorporeal Movement.** Vos Nosu can move through other creatures and objects as if they were difficult terrain. He takes 5 (1d10) force damage if he ends his turn inside an object.

**Legendary Resistance (2/Day).** If Vos Nosu fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.

### Actions

**Eye Lightning.** *Ranged Spell Attack:* +9 to hit, range 120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 28 (8d6) lightning damage.

**Summon Demons (Recharge 5-6).** Vos Nosu summons 1d4+1 **Dretches** or 1 **Shadow Demon** that appear in unoccupied spaces within 60 feet. The demons act on their own initiative and obey Vos Nosu's commands.

**Spellcasting.** Vos Nosu casts one of the following spells (spell save DC 17):
- At will: *mage hand*, *minor illusion*
- 3/day each: *counterspell*, *dispel magic*, *hold person*
- 1/day each: *banishment*, *circle of death*

### Legendary Actions

Vos Nosu can take 2 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn.

**Eye Bolt.** Vos Nosu makes one Eye Lightning attack.
**Fade (Costs 2 Actions).** Vos Nosu becomes invisible until the start of his next turn or until he attacks.

---

## Breaking the Loop

To permanently end the time loop, the players must:

1. Defeat or banish Vos Nosu
2. Reunite all 32 Codex books in their proper places
3. Destroy or disable Finaeo Vineca's time loop device

> The pedestal crumbles. The face of Vos Nosu screams silently as it dissolves into motes of light. Around you, the catacombs shudder. Somewhere above, a clock strikes ten—a time that should be impossible. The loop is broken.

*Estimated play time: 12-16 hours (3-4 sessions)*
